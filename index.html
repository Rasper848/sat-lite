Sat-lite

Amateur satellites and rig control on iPhone

Sat-lite is a native iPhone and Apple Watch app for amateur satellite work and Icom radio control. Track upcoming passes, follow Doppler on a supported radio, aim with the AR view, and use FT8, FT4, PSK, SSTV, WSPR, APRS, Winlink, POTA and optional QRZ Logbook upload from the phone — with no PC in the loop.

The app is free; an optional tip may be offered as an in-app purchase, processed by Apple. Pass predictions, the catalog, news and the AR view work without a radio. Tracking, rig control and on-air modes need a supported Icom radio. Transmitting requires a licensed amateur radio operator.

Supported radios

  • IC-9700, IC-7610 and IC-705 over the radio’s network remote server
  • ID-52A and ID-52A PLUS through an ESP32 Bluetooth bridge (ID52-Bridge)
  • ID-5200 over built-in Bluetooth for frequency and mode (this link does not carry audio, PTT or APRS)

An Apple Watch companion shows the next-pass countdown and live azimuth and elevation. It is display only and never controls the radio.
